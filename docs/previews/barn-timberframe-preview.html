Five timber-frame barns

Modeled after the reference photos: a flared gambrel roof, an exposed king-post truss under the ridge, and cross-braced plank doors — the three details that read as "timber-frame party barn" from across a field. Three keep the full picture (porches, dormer, windows); two strip everything but the signature silhouette down to the fewest triangles that still read correctly.
