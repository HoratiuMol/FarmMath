A rocky den sits on whichever border edge the player places it; every so often a bear pops her head out to look around, then ducks back in.

Palette & materials reuse

Nothing new here except the bear's own fur tones — everything else is pulled straight from assets already shipping in FarmGridCanvas.kt, so the den reads as built from the same world as the river and the fence.

Ink outline — shared by every asset
Boulder grey — river spring's drawRock, reused as-is
Packed-earth mound — fence post brown
Grass cap — river reed green
Bear fur — new, warm brown so she doesn't read as "the cow"
Bear snout — new, tan

Try the 4 edge buttons and "Expand grid" — the den re-anchors to side + alongFraction exactly like the river, so it always stays just outside the new fence line.

Real pacing (not shown at preview speed): the bear is hidden for a random 1–5 real minutes, same kill-safe timestamp pattern as CowHunger/AnimalLifespan — a stored lastDuckedAtTimestamp + a randomly-rolled nextPeekDelayMs (60_000–300_000), with phase/progress recomputed from elapsed time every frame rather than a live countdown, so she doesn't lose her place if the app is backgrounded mid-peek. Rise/hold/ sink (≈0.5s / 1.8s / 0.5s) stay fixed regardless of the random gap. Purely decorative, same as the river — no tap target, no gameplay effect.
