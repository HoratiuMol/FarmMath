Farm Math Builder · Asset Preview

Farm cow, design options

Four directions for the wandering, non-interactive cow (decoration only — no tap target, no gameplay effect). Each one auto-walks back and forth on a small patch of the game's actual ground green so you can judge scale, readability at tile size, and how the walk cycle reads. Pick a letter and I'll build it into the real game.

ALow-poly 3D

Flat-shaded cuboid mesh, same camera/shading recipe as the barn and fence (BarnMesh/FenceMesh) — most visually consistent with the rest of the farm, and the easiest to port 1:1 into Kotlin since it's just more cuboids, no new pipeline.

BChibi sticker

Big rounded head, soft body, oversized eyes — a cute 2D cartoon sprite. Reads friendliest for a kids' math app, but is a different visual language than the barn/fence's low-poly 3D.

CPixel art

Small retro pixel sprite, two-frame walk cycle. Very cheap to render (no shape math, just a color grid), but is a third distinct visual language next to the 3D buildings and the 2D crop tiles.

DFlat paper-cutout

Geometric flat-color shapes with a soft drop shadow, no outlines — same "flat" spirit as the crop-tile art (drawWheatTile) rather than the 3D barn. Minimal, calm, quick to scan at small size.

All four walk on a loop within a fixed patch — this is only a design/scale preview, not the real wander logic (the shipped version will roam a wider area of the buildable grid on a timer-driven path, still non-interactive).
