Five classic barns

One template this time, not five: a rectangular body, a gable (or gambrel) roof, a hayloft door, a sliding door, a stone footing — the shape every player already reads as "barn." All five share that same simple mesh; what tells them apart is material, trim, and the small working details a real barn accretes over decades.
